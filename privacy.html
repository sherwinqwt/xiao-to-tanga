Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 3, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how XIAO TO TANGA ("the Bot") collects, uses, stores, and handles information when users interact with it on Discord.

2. Information We Collect

To provide its moderation and server-management features, the Bot may collect and store the following information:

  • Discord User IDs.
  • Discord Server (Guild) IDs.
  • Moderator IDs associated with moderation actions.
  • Warning records and moderation history.
  • Moderation action types, such as bans, kicks, mutes, and warnings.
  • Moderation reasons and timestamps, when provided or recorded by the Bot.
  • Server, channel, and role information needed to provide the Bot's features.

3. How We Use Information

Information is used only to operate and maintain the Bot's features, including:

  • Recording and managing moderation actions.
  • Keeping track of user warnings and moderation history.
  • Managing server channels and roles when requested.
  • Providing server information and user information.
  • Maintaining the Bot's functionality and troubleshooting issues.

4. Data Storage and Retention

Some moderation information is stored in a database to maintain moderation records and support the Bot's features.

Information is retained only for as long as it is necessary to provide the Bot's functionality. Requests for deletion can be submitted to the Bot developer.

The developer should update this policy to specify the database provider and actual retention periods.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell users' personal information. Information is not shared with third parties for advertising or unrelated purposes.

The Bot operates through Discord's platform and API. Discord processes information according to its own Privacy Policy.

6. Data Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect stored information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. However, no storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Data Deletion Requests

Users may contact the Bot developer to request access to or deletion of information associated with their Discord account. Requests will be reviewed and handled in accordance with applicable requirements.

8. Children's Privacy

The Bot is not intended to collect information from children below the minimum age required to use Discord in their jurisdiction.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the Bot's features or data practices change. Updated versions will be published on this page.
